# BOUNCING HERO
Official repository for the video game ***Bouncing Hero*** by Avaneesh Prabha and Medhansh Pappula, with uniquely designed characters and arts.

## About
Take control of a courageous bouncy ball in an action-packed roguelike adventure where every ricochet and slam is an attack against invading foes. Stack powerful spells and abilities by beating bosses and mobs that help to smash through waves of enemies and protect the kingdom's bouncy castle from total takeover!

## How to Play
- ("MOVE LEFT / RIGHT", "[A] / [D] or [LEFT-ARROW KEY] / [RIGHT-ARROW KEY]")
- ("JUMP", "[SPACE] or [W] or [UP-ARROW KEY]")
- ("SLOW-MO", "[E] (Uses Energy)"),
- ("AIR DASH & SLAM", "In mid-air: Hold [RIGHT-CLICK] & drag toward target, release to launch"),
- ("SUPER DASH", "Hold air aim for 2 seconds (Unlocked after tutorial)"),
- ("SHIELD ABILITY", "[Uses 2 Energy Points] (Blocks wolf hits for 4 seconds)"),
- ("GUST PULSE ABILITY", "[Uses 3 Energy Points] (Knocks back and damages nearby wolves)"),
- ("PAUSE", "[P] or Top-Right Pause Button"),
